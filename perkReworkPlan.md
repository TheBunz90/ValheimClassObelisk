would like to re-work existing perks to all follow a similar basic perk flow:

Lvl. 10 - small extra damage buff to class weapons. (could be flat damage or increased attack speed)
- These should not be anything more than a 5-10% increase in any stat. Enough to feel like you earned something but not too meaningful / exciting.
Lvl. 20 - Some kind of class passive. (Magic Arrow / Colossus are good examples)
- These should all be mostly quality of life improvements and not add any real combat advantage.
Lvl. 30 - Some kind of class combat buff. (Riposte / Arrow Slinger)
- This should be where the class "personality" really reveals itself. The buffs should give the player a meaningful combat buff. 
Lvl. 40 - Elemental Attribute added to all weapons. (Venom Coating / Cold Steel are good examples)
- Continuation of class personality, something that compliments the Lvl. 30 buff or compliments the typical play style of the weapon.
Lvl. 50 - Medium sized damage buff. (Rage, Reverb!, Twist The Knife are good examples)
- This should be a big pay off for reaching max level. Some form of really impactful damage buff. 10-20% increase in weapon damage or attack speed that really feels like you're a master at this weapon now.

Currently a lot of weapons are sort of neglected with the current perk system, namely most all of the 2 handed weapons. Most of the 2H weapons are included in a partilcular grouping but the perks I have now are designed more with 1H weapons in mind. I'd like to update the existing perk system to have specific perks for 2H weapons vs. 1h weapons.

Crusher for example, it's Lvl. 30 perk only applies to Heavy Attacks. But the 2H hammers that are included in the Crusher category do not have Heavy Attacks, so this perk just does nothing for them. I'd like to have a different perk that applies specifically to 2H Hammers for the lvl. 30 perk to make it still meaningful, and allow players to feel like they can still use ANY weapon and get full benefits from this system. Other scenarios like this would be greatswords, halberds, and crossbows. (I'm aware there are several magic weapons that also do not get any benefits from an existing class but I will want to add more classes down the line to address those)
